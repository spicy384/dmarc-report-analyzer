DMARC Report Analyzer

DMARC reports from your mailboxes, explained

Connect your reports mailbox

Step 1 of 4

Nothing is reading reports yet. This walkthrough connects the mailbox that receives your DMARC reports (the rua= address). It takes a few minutes.

What you need depends on where the mailbox lives:

  1. Microsoft 365: an Entra app registration with the Graph application permission Mail.Read and admin consent, its tenant and client IDs, and a client secret or certificate.
  2. Google Workspace: a service account key (JSON) with domain-wide delegation for the read-only Gmail scope, and the mailbox address.
  3. Amazon SES: the S3 bucket your receipt rule delivers to, its region, and an access key allowed to list and read it.
  4. IMAP or POP3: the server name, a username and a password (an app password if two-factor is on).

The README has the setup steps for each. The mailbox is only ever read: nothing is deleted, moved or marked. Everything you enter here can be changed later under Mailbox sync.

          Backup and maintenance
          
        
      
          
      
          
        
          
          
          Backup
          
          
          
            Downloads a .tar.gz with a consistent copy of the database plus users.json and
            mailboxes.json. It contains password hashes, TOTP secrets and mailbox credentials, so
            store it somewhere private. Sessions and GeoLite2 files are not included.
          
          
          
          
            
          
          
        
          
        
          
          
          Restore
          
          
          
            Replaces every report, record, label, alert and setting with the backup's, and
            overwrites the accounts and mailboxes. Nothing is merged. If your own account is not in
            the backup you will be signed out.
          
          
          
          
          
            
          
          
          
          
        
          
        
          
          
          Re-process stored XML
          
          
          
            Parses every stored report again with the current parser and rewrites its records, so a
            parser improvement applies to reports you already have. Reports rolled up by retention
            no longer have their XML and are skipped.
          
          
          
          
            
          
          
          
          
        
          
      
          
    
          

    
          
      
          
        
          Notifications
          
        
      
          
      
          
        Post new alerts and the weekly summary to an incoming webhook: a Microsoft Teams workflow
        ("Post to a channel when a webhook request is received"), a Slack app's incoming webhook, or
        any endpoint that accepts JSON. The URL is a credential; it is stored but never shown again.
      
          
      
          
        
        
        
        
        
      
          
      
      
      
          
        
        
        
      
          
      
          
    
          

    
          
      
          
        
          Monitoring
          
        
      
          
      
          
        Once a day the analyzer looks up the DMARC, SPF, DKIM, MTA-STS and TLS-RPT records (and
        the MTA-STS policy file) of every domain that reported in the last 90 days and raises an
        alert when one changes or disappears (the
        first snapshot is the baseline). After each sync and every hour it also checks that
        reporting services which used to send regularly are still sending, and that reports
        keep arriving at all. Those two alerts clear themselves when the data resumes.
